About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT). My research is in extremal combinatorics, with a particular focus on extremal set theory.

I study intersecting and cross-intersecting families, the structure of extremal families, and stability problems. I am also interested in matching problems and shifting methods.

I received my Ph.D. from Hunan University, advised by Yuejian Peng. Previously, I studied at Central South University under the supervision of Weijun Liu. My earlier work includes algebraic graph theory and matrix inequalities.

Research interests

  • Intersecting and cross-intersecting families
  • Extremal structures, stability, and diversity
  • Matching problems and shifting methods
